When Do U.S. Monetary Policy Surprises Matter for Australian Borrowing Costs?
Replication notebook for the paper examining the transmission of U.S. monetary policy surprises (Jarociński-Karadi shocks) to Australian Government Securities yields, 2013–2026.
This notebook covers: data cleaning and merging, descriptive statistics, stationarity testing, the baseline OLS/HAC and Local Projections specifications, all robustness checks, and the secondary analysis of non-resident AGS holdings.
All data sources are documented in the paper's Data Sources section.# U.S.-monetary-policy--AGS-Yields-
